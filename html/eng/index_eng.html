PREMIUM CLASSIC

CORSINI PANETTONE, THE BEST TRADITION

Our Premium Classic line is a refined reinterpretation of Corsini's historic leavened cake collection and a tribute to the art of traditional Italian baking. These timeless recipes enhance the atmosphere of the festive season. Each cake is wrapped in fine-quality paper that evokes the atmosphere of premium pastry shops.
They are precious gifts that help rediscover the beauty of simple gestures and authentic flavours.
Since 1921, the Corsini Panettone has been a story of family and passion. Select ingredients, daily care of the sourdough starter, and traditional hand-shaping. After baking, Corsini cakes are also left to stand for all the time they need to achieve their unique softness. No preservatives or artificial flavouring are used.
Our panettoni weigh 920g.

CLASSIC PANDORO

CORSINI PANDORO, THE BEST TRADITION

The first exclusive ingredient here is our passion for quality. Then there is our loving care for the sourdough starter, that we have always treated like a child at Corsini.
The ingredients are all premium-quality, and we observe the strictest rules of traditional cake-making, which include patient, meticulous kneading and hand-shaping the dough.
We then add the magic of our historic family oven, which is perfect for baking, and finally... there is the long, natural standing time that ensures our Pandoro is even softer and tastier.
This is how this small masterpiece of precious goodness, the Corsini Pandoro, is created. It is still made today, exactly as it once was, and since 1921, we have been proud to put our family signature on it.
No preservatives or artificial flavouring are used.
Weight: 1kg.

PAN DI CORTE

WITH TUSCAN PGI OLIVE OIL

SOURDOUGH MEETS THE REFINEMENT OF “TUSCAN PGI" EXTRA VIRGIN OLIVE OIL
Since 1988, “Tuscan PGI” olive oil has carried the PGI – or Geographically Protected – hallmark as an indication of its authenticity and close bond with the land and culture of our region.
The inclusion of this refined ingredient has led to the creation of our delicate, but incredibly delicious Pan di Corte, a leavened cake with candied orange peel and sultanas, but no butter.

No preservatives or artificial flavouring are used.
Weight: 750g.
